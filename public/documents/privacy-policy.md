# ForceRefresh Privacy Policy

**Version:** 2.0
**Effective Date:** 31 March 2026
**Previous version:** 1.0 (17 December 2025)

## 1. Who Collects Your Information

This Privacy Policy explains how **the ForceRefresh Team** ("we," "us," "our") collects, uses, and shares your information when you use our Service.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide

- Account details (name, email address)
- Social media credentials (for publishing)
- Content you create, upload, or schedule
- Communications with our support team

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically

- Device information (type, operating system)
- IP address and browser type
- Usage data (features used, session length)
- Cookies and similar technologies for essential operation

### 2.3 Information from Third Parties

When you connect social accounts, we receive basic profile information per those platforms' API policies.

### 2.4 AI Feature Interaction Data

When you use the AI Stock Images library, we automatically collect:

- Search terms and filter selections you use when browsing the library
- Which AI Stock Images you preview, select, insert into designs, or remove from designs
- Frequency and patterns of AI feature usage within your account
- Feedback signals such as image ratings or reports you submit
- Technical metadata associated with your interactions, including timestamps and session identifiers

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

- Provide, maintain, and improve the Service
- Communicate with you about updates, security, or support
- Analyze usage trends to enhance user experience
- Prevent fraud, abuse, and comply with legal obligations
- Analyze AI feature interaction data (as described in Section 2.4) to improve library curation, search relevance, and the overall quality of AI Stock Images available to users
- Evaluate aggregated, de-identified usage patterns to assess the performance of the AI systems that generate our stock image library; this analysis does not identify you personally

## 4. AI-Generated Content and Your Data

### 4.1 Use of Your Data in Relation to AI

ForceRefresh does not use your personal information, your User Content, or your account-level AI interaction data to directly train, fine-tune, or build AI models. The AI Stock Images in our library were pre-generated using a commercially-licensed AI image generation tool operating under a permissive license.

Aggregated and de-identified data about how users collectively interact with AI Stock Images (for example, which categories of images are frequently browsed or selected) may be used to inform curation decisions and library expansion.

### 4.2 AI Image Generation

The images in our library were generated using a commercially-licensed AI tool. Your personal information is not shared with the AI tool provider in connection with image generation or delivery.

### 4.3 Your Opt-Out Rights

If you are located in a jurisdiction that grants you the right to object to or opt out of processing of your personal data for purposes of AI system improvement, you may submit such a request to **privacy@forcerefresh.io**. We will honor opt-out requests to the extent required by applicable law.

Opting out of AI improvement processing does not affect your ability to use the AI Stock Images library or any other feature of the Service.

## 5. How We Share Your Information

- **With Social Platforms:** Only to publish content at your explicit direction
- **With Service Providers:** Trusted vendors (hosting, analytics, payment processors) who assist our operations under confidentiality agreements
- **For Legal Reasons:** If required by law or to protect rights and safety

**We do not sell your personal information.**

## 6. Data Retention

### 6.1 General Retention

We retain your personal information only as long as necessary to:

- Provide the Service to you
- Comply with legal obligations
- Resolve disputes
- Enforce our agreements

You can request account deletion at any time.

### 6.2 AI Interaction Data Retention

AI feature interaction data (Section 2.4) is retained as follows:

- Detailed interaction logs (individual image selections, search queries) are retained for no longer than 24 months from collection, after which they are either deleted or aggregated into anonymized datasets
- Anonymized, aggregated datasets derived from AI interaction data do not contain personal identifiers and are not subject to deletion upon account closure
- If you request account deletion, we will delete or anonymize your AI interaction data within 30 days, except where retention is required by legal obligation

## 7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

- Access your personal data
- Correct inaccurate data
- Delete your data
- Object to or restrict processing
- Data portability

To exercise these rights, contact: privacy@forcerefresh.io

## 8. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data. However, no internet transmission is 100% secure.

## 9. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed in countries with different data protection laws. Where required, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place.

## 10. Children's Privacy

Our Service is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

## 11. Cookies

We use necessary cookies for site functionality. You can control non-essential cookies through your browser settings.

## 12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. We will notify you of significant changes via email or in-app notice.
