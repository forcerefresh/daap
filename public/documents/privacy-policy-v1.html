ForceRefresh Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 17th December 2025

1. Who Collects Your Information

This Privacy Policy explains how the ForceRefresh Team ("we," "us," "our") collects, uses, and shares your information when you use our Service.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Account details (name, email address)
  • Social media credentials (for publishing)
  • Content you create, upload, or schedule
  • Communications with our support team

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device information (type, operating system)
  • IP address and browser type
  • Usage data (features used, session length)
  • Cookies and similar technologies for essential operation

2.3 Information from Third Parties

When you connect social accounts, we receive basic profile information per those platforms' API policies.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Service
  • Communicate with you about updates, security, or support
  • Analyze usage trends to enhance user experience
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, and comply with legal obligations

4. How We Share Your Information

  • With Social Platforms: Only to publish content at your explicit direction
  • With Service Providers: Trusted vendors (hosting, analytics, payment processors) who assist our operations under confidentiality agreements
  • For Legal Reasons: If required by law or to protect rights and safety

We do not sell your personal information.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only as long as necessary to:

  • Provide the Service to you
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Resolve disputes
  • Enforce our agreements

You can request account deletion at any time.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your data
  • Object to or restrict processing
  • Data portability

To exercise these rights, contact: privacy@forcerefresh.io

7. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data. However, no internet transmission is 100% secure.

8. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed in countries with different data protection laws. Where required, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place.

9. Children's Privacy

Our Service is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

10. Cookies

We use necessary cookies for site functionality. You can control non-essential cookies through your browser settings.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. We will notify you of significant changes via email or in-app notice.

12. Contact Us

For privacy-related questions or requests:

Email: privacy@forcerefresh.io
